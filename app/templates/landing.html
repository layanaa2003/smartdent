Read our approach

One x-ray, four careful steps.

Scroll through what happens to a panoramic x-ray from the moment it is uploaded to the moment your dentist talks you through it.

STEP 1 OF 4 · UPLOAD

Your x-ray is added to your record

Your dentist takes a panoramic x-ray as usual and uploads it to your patient record in SmartDent. Nothing changes in the chair.

STEP 2 OF 4 · SCAN

SmartDent reads the image

A detection model trained on de-identified panoramic x-rays looks for four things: caries, periapical lesions, impacted teeth and bone loss.

STEP 3 OF 4 · HIGHLIGHT

Possible findings are boxed

Each possible finding gets a box, a label and a confidence score. These are prompts for a professional, not results for you.

STEP 4 OF 4 · REVIEW

Your dentist makes the call

Your dentist checks the whole image, agrees or disagrees with each box, and adds their own judgement. Then they explain the plan and can send you the x-ray and a PDF report.

Good questions, plain answers.

If something isn’t covered here, ask your dentist at your appointment. They can show you exactly what SmartDent highlighted.

Does SmartDent diagnose me?
No. SmartDent highlights areas on an x-ray that may deserve a closer look. A qualified dentist reviews every finding, agrees or disagrees with it, and makes the diagnosis. AI output is advisory only.
Which x-rays does it work with?
Two-dimensional panoramic x-rays (also called OPGs), uploaded as JPEG or PNG. Periapical, bitewing and 3D (CBCT) scans are not supported yet.
What does it look for?
Four things: caries (decay), periapical lesions (infection or inflammation at a root tip), impacted teeth, and bone loss. Anything else is left to your dentist’s own reading of the image.
What does the confidence score mean?
It is how sure the model is that its box contains that finding. A high score is not a diagnosis, and a low score is not an all-clear. Your dentist decides.
Who can see my images?
The dentist responsible for your care. Practice admins manage dentist accounts and can see the patient list. Your dentist can email you your x-ray and report, or give you a copy.
Will this mean more x-rays or more radiation?
No. SmartDent reads the images your dentist would take anyway. Whether you need an x-ray, and how often, is still your dentist’s decision.
